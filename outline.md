Background
Your development team is already using AI tooling day to day - GitHub Copilot for Business
is embedded in the workflow, and individuals are beginning to explore what is possible with
more advanced patterns such as agents and MCP. The challenge now is not adoption but
alignment: moving from a team where everyone has their own approach to one with shared
vocabulary, agreed standards, and a clear picture of where this technology is heading.
This proposal is informed by a conversation with Jonathan Modley, David Lammie and Tim
Hawk-Walker, and builds on a prior working relationship with the team across Laravel,
testing, and security. The goal is an engagement that meets the team where they are -
technically capable, curious, but without a unified process - and leaves them with one.
Objectives
By the end of the engagement, participants will be able to:
• Apply a consistent, principled approach to AI-assisted development across the full
SDLC
• Use GitHub Copilot effectively in a Laravel/PHP context, beyond basic
autocompletion
• Write prompts and structure context deliberately - treating context as a first-class
concern
• Identify where AI assistance adds genuine value and where human judgement is
irreplaceable
• Understand the security implications of AI tooling and apply appropriate guardrails
• Articulate a shared team standard for AI use, reducing ad-hoc variation
• Recognise what is coming next - agents, MCP, multi-agent workflows - and how it
connects to their current work
Day 1 — Core Programme
A full day delivered via Teams, with a mix of instruction, demonstration, and hands-on
exercises. Sessions are designed to be interactive and grounded in Laravel/PHP examples
throughout. The day is structured to move from foundations to standards to a forwardlooking close.
Module Topics Covered
Morning — Foundations &
Standards
Shared vocabulary; how LLMs actually work (enough to
reason about behaviour); prompt engineering principles;
context as code - structuring prompts and project context
deliberately; garbage in / garbage out in practice
Late Morning — Copilot in
Depth
Beyond tab completion - inline chat, slash commands,
Copilot in the terminal; effective use within a Laravel
codebase; working with Copilot on routing, controllers,
Eloquent, and testing; managing suggestions critically rather
than accepting passively
Afternoon — SDLC
Integration
Applying AI across the full development lifecycle:
requirements and planning, code generation, refactoring,
code review, documentation, and test writing; establishing
team conventions; security guardrails and what not to send to
an AI model
Late Afternoon — The Art
of the Possible
Introduction to agents and agentic workflows; Model Context
Protocol (MCP) - what it is and why it matters; multi-agent
patterns; a glimpse of TDD with an agent harness; where the
Laravel ecosystem is heading with AI tooling
Follow-Up Session (approx. 4 weeks later)
A half-day session scheduled approximately four weeks after the core day. By this point the
team will have had time to apply what they have learned in a real codebase, and will have
formed their own questions and encountered their own friction.
The follow-up is structured as a combination of retrospective and deeper dive:
• Review: what has the team adopted, what has worked, what has not?
• Common patterns and anti-patterns that have emerged
• Targeted Q&A based on real work done since the core day
• One deeper-dive topic chosen by the team (e.g. a specific Copilot feature, prompt
patterns for their domain, or an expanded look at agents)
This session is deliberately adaptive. Its value comes from being anchored in what the team
has actually experienced, not in delivering more content for its own sake.

Approach and Delivery Style
All sessions are delivered remotely via Microsoft Teams. Instruction is kept practical -
examples are drawn from Laravel and PHP throughout, not generic code snippets. The aim
is always to leave participants with something they can use the following morning.
Given the spectrum of opinions on the team about what AI can and cannot do, sessions are
designed to be honest: the goal is calibrated confidence, not hype. Time is built in for
discussion, challenge, and for the team to surface their own questions.
All materials - exercises, reference guides, prompt templates - are provided and remain with
the team after the engagement.
